Tower of Rings Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Review draft for the planned ad-enabled Android release. Confirm the final consent provider, advertising configuration, and in-app privacy controls before publishing this policy.

Who we are

Tower of Rings is provided by G.H.T.P Games. For privacy questions or requests, contact ghtpgames@gmail.com.

Game progress and preferences

The game stores your selected ring count, unlocked levels, music and sound settings, and rating-reminder preferences on your device. Rating preferences include a limited launch counter, the first-launch date, and whether you declined reminders. Our game code does not upload these values to a G.H.T.P Games server.

You do not need to create a player account. The game does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photographs, microphone recordings, or precise GPS location to play.

Unity technology and advertising

The planned advertising integration uses Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads to display occasional advertisements between levels. Unity technology and advertising services may process device and advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location inferred from the IP address, device and app information, diagnostics, and interactions with advertisements. The information processed depends on the services enabled, device settings, and applicable privacy choices.

These services use information to deliver and measure advertisements, operate and improve their services, and detect fraud. Personalized advertising may involve using information across apps or services, subject to applicable choices and requirements.

Read Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy for its processing practices, recipients, international transfers, retention, and privacy rights. This game policy does not replace Unity's policy.

G.H.T.P Games does not operate an account or cloud-save service for this game. Advertising partners process information through their SDKs; this is separate from the progress saved locally on your device.

Privacy choices

The game integrates Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request applicable privacy requirements, present configured consent or opt-out messages, and record privacy choices. Google's handling of information through this service is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

The Options menu and pause menu include a Privacy choices control that is shown when UMP reports that a privacy-options entry point is required. A separate Privacy policy control opens this page. Local game settings and privacy choices are different: clearing game progress does not send a deletion request to Google or Unity.

Advertising is not yet initialized in the development build covered by this draft. Before the ad-enabled release, the consent flow, any applicable opt-out signals, and their handling by LevelPlay and each ad network must be configured and verified. Declining optional advertising consent will not prevent access to the puzzle itself. Whether advertisements can be served after a choice depends on the selected services and their requirements.

Depending on your location, advertising disclosures may constitute a sale or sharing of personal information or targeted advertising. Applicable opt-out choices must be available through the release's privacy controls. Android also provides advertising-identifier controls; their availability and wording depend on your device and Android version.

Support email

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever information you include. We use that information to respond and resolve your request. Please do not send sensitive information that is unnecessary for support. Email is processed through our email provider, Gmail.

Retention and deletion

Local progress and preferences remain in app storage until they are reset or removed. You can clear app storage using Android's app settings; doing so also removes saved progress. Device backup and restore settings may affect whether local settings are restored.

We retain support correspondence only as needed to address the inquiry, handle follow-up issues, and meet applicable obligations. Consent-management and advertising-service data is retained under the providers' policies; clearing game storage does not by itself delete data already held by Google or an advertising provider.

Privacy rights and contact

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, or opt out of certain advertising disclosures. Contact ghtpgames@gmail.com for requests involving information held by G.H.T.P Games. For data held by Unity, its linked policy explains available request methods. Identity verification may be needed, and rights can have exceptions. Where applicable, you may complain to your local data-protection authority.

Children

Tower of Rings is intended as a general-audience puzzle game and is not specifically directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to G.H.T.P Games, contact us. If the game's intended audience changes, its privacy and advertising setup must be reviewed before release.

Changes

We will update this policy when our practices change and revise the date above. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request a new privacy choice.
